# Deep Mediatization — Platforms, AI, and Creative Work

A qualitative research workflow examining how platforms and AI reshape performing and screen arts.

- Combines deductive and emergent interview coding with within-case and cross-case synthesis.
- Verifies coded quotations against source text and aligns interview themes with survey questions.
- Includes the interview guide, survey workbook, and a previously exported analysis workbook.
- Adds preview-first execution, configurable model selection, bounded transcript chunks, and model-aware caches.
- Tests the analysis-to-export workflow with a synthetic interview and mocked responses.

**Reproducibility:** Original interview transcripts are not included. Regenerating the research analysis requires those inputs and API credentials. AI-generated coding and interpretations require researcher review.
